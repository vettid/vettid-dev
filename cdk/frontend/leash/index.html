LEASH

Lightweight Encrypted Agent Secret Handling

A scoped, time-bound, version-bound delegation token. AI agents act on your behalf using only what you grant — never the underlying secret. Mint one for Demo Alice's agent, then throw the worst you've got at it. The validator rejects every attack and tells you why.

Test your own agent against the live validator

      

        Spawn a 30-minute session. The page gives you a CLI one-liner — run it in
        your terminal and your agent will sign a verification envelope with its
        Ed25519 key and post it here. Verification chain appears below in real
        time.
      

      

        You'll need a real LEASH JWT from your VettID vault. Two ways to mint one:
      

      
    
        
  • 
          Agent-initiated (recommended):
          vettid-agent leash mint --scope profile.email:read on a paired
          agent will publish an approval request to your phone; tap Approve and the
          JWT lands in the CLI's stdout — nothing to copy by hand.
        
    • 
        
  • 
          Phone-initiated: in the VettID app, open
          Settings → Agent Connections → your agent → Mint LEASH,
          paste the agent's Ed25519 pubkey (from vettid-agent leash pubkey),
          pick scopes, hit Mint.
